\section{Related work}
\paragraph{Continuous and discrete paradigms.}
Continuous language diffusion evolves real-valued embeddings or latents through a noise process or flow; discrete diffusion evolves categorical token states, often by masking and unmasking. The distinction concerns the state and generative mechanism, not whether time is continuous. Plaid develops likelihood-based embedding diffusion \citep{plaid}; SEDD and MDLM develop discrete objectives \citep{sedd,mdlm}. Diffusion of Thoughts evaluates both families on reasoning \citep{dot}. We compare continuous methods as our primary methodological peers and tabulate discrete systems separately. Hybrid methods are identified by their actual operations rather than their names.

\paragraph{Continuous representations and reasoning.}
LangFlow and RePlaid study continuous embedding geometry, schedules, and likelihood training \citep{langflow,replaid}. On GSM8K, $\mathbb S$-FLM uses hyperspherical transport and top-1 velocity; FMLM+ combines two-time flow maps with masking-style schedules and posterior token refinement; MLFM combines continuous corruption with masking, guidance, and online token promotion \citep{hyperspherical,posterior,mlfm}. Their auxiliary mechanisms and NFE dependence matter alongside accuracy. We build on ELF's contextual embedding flow, shared decoder, self-conditioning, and SCCFG \citep{elf}, adding teacher-predictive compression, whitened multilayer targets, staged prompt adaptation, and mathematical post-training. Our answer trajectory has no discrete token commitment or autoregression between blocks. LaDiR and LaDi-RL instead diffuse latent thoughts while retaining an autoregressive language decoder \citep{ladir,ladirl}.

\paragraph{Discrete reasoning and reward optimization.}
SMDM, LLaDA, and Dream scale discrete diffusion \citep{smdm,llada,dream}. Block diffusion, SDAR, and Fast-dLLM v2 combine autoregression across blocks with refinement within them; OPDLM and TIDE study distillation \citep{blockdiffusion,sdar,fastv2,opdlm,tide}. These are a distinct performance reference. Reward learning is also established: d1, LLaDA 1.5, wd1, d2, SPG, ESPO, and GDSD develop diffusion policy optimization or reward-guided self-distillation \citep{d1,llada15,wd1,d2,spg,espo,gdsd}. We adapt DiffusionNFT's forward-process objective \citep{diffusionnft} to continuous reasoning latents and a learned prompt conditioner, evaluating its effects on correctness, coverage, and diversity.

\paragraph{Sampling and inference resources.}
Discrete samplers and distillation methods alter quality versus cost \citep{fastdllm,cdlm,t3d}, while confidence, exploration, and intermediate-state voting affect answer selection \citep{qualityexploration,midtruth}. We study denoising depth versus independent draws using both self-consistency and pass@$k$ \citep{selfconsistency,codex}. Our comparison records the parameters used at each step separately from one-time conditioning and terminal decoding. A vocabulary head can be recurrent even in a continuous model, and a sampling step can require multiple guided evaluations. Appendix~\ref{app:literature} retains authors' reported accuracies and these inference distinctions.
